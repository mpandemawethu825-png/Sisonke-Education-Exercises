Question 1

456 ÷ 8 = ?

✅ Solution: 456 ÷ 8 = 57

1 Divide the first digit(s): How many times does 8 go into 4? Zero times (4 is smaller than 8), so we look at the first two digits: 45.
2 8 goes into 45: 8 × 5 = 40. So 8 goes into 45 five times with a remainder.
5 ----- 8 | 456 -40 ← (8 × 5 = 40) --- 56
3 Subtract and bring down: 45 - 40 = 5. Bring down the next digit (6) to make 56.
4 8 goes into 56: 8 × 7 = 56. So 8 goes into 56 exactly 7 times.
57 ----- 8 | 456 -40 --- 56 -56 ← (8 × 7 = 56) --- 0 ← No remainder!
5 Final Answer: 456 ÷ 8 = 57 with no remainder.

Question 2

735 ÷ 5 = ?

✅ Solution: 735 ÷ 5 = 147

1 Divide the first digit: How many times does 5 go into 7? 5 × 1 = 5.
1 ----- 5 | 735 -5 ← (5 × 1 = 5) --- 23
2 Subtract and bring down: 7 - 5 = 2. Bring down the 3 to make 23.
3 5 goes into 23: 5 × 4 = 20. So 5 goes into 23 four times.
14 ----- 5 | 735 -5 --- 23 -20 ← (5 × 4 = 20) --- 35
4 Subtract and bring down: 23 - 20 = 3. Bring down the 5 to make 35.
5 5 goes into 35: 5 × 7 = 35. Perfect!
147 ----- 5 | 735 -5 --- 23 -20 --- 35 -35 ← (5 × 7 = 35) --- 0
6 Final Answer: 735 ÷ 5 = 147 with no remainder.

Question 3

892 ÷ 4 = ?

✅ Solution: 892 ÷ 4 = 223

1 Divide the first digit: 4 goes into 8 exactly 2 times (4 × 2 = 8).
2 ----- 4 | 892 -8 ← (4 × 2 = 8) --- 09
2 Subtract and bring down: 8 - 8 = 0. Bring down the 9 to make 09 (which is just 9).
3 4 goes into 9: 4 × 2 = 8. So 4 goes into 9 two times.
22 ----- 4 | 892 -8 --- 09 -8 ← (4 × 2 = 8) --- 12
4 Subtract and bring down: 9 - 8 = 1. Bring down the 2 to make 12.
5 4 goes into 12: 4 × 3 = 12. Perfect fit!
223 ----- 4 | 892 -8 --- 09 -8 --- 12 -12 ← (4 × 3 = 12) --- 0
6 Final Answer: 892 ÷ 4 = 223 with no remainder.

Question 4

1,248 ÷ 12 = ?

✅ Solution: 1,248 ÷ 12 = 104

1 Divide by two digits: How many times does 12 go into 1? Zero times. How about 12? Once! (12 × 1 = 12)
1 ----- 12 | 1248 -12 ← (12 × 1 = 12) --- 04
2 Subtract and bring down: 12 - 12 = 0. Bring down the 4 to make 04 (which is 4).
3 12 goes into 4: It doesn't! 4 is smaller than 12, so we put a 0 in the quotient and bring down the next digit.
10 ----- 12 | 1248 -12 --- 04 -0 ← (12 × 0 = 0) --- 48
4 Bring down the 8: Now we have 48.
5 12 goes into 48: 12 × 4 = 48. Perfect!
104 ----- 12 | 1248 -12 --- 04 -0 --- 48 -48 ← (12 × 4 = 48) --- 0
6 Final Answer: 1,248 ÷ 12 = 104 with no remainder.

Question 5

2,835 ÷ 15 = ?

✅ Solution: 2,835 ÷ 15 = 189

1 Start dividing: 15 doesn't go into 2. Does it go into 28? Yes! 15 × 1 = 15.
1 ----- 15 | 2835 -15 ← (15 × 1 = 15) --- 133
2 Subtract and bring down: 28 - 15 = 13. Bring down the 3 to make 133.
3 15 goes into 133: Let's see... 15 × 8 = 120, and 15 × 9 = 135 (too big). So it's 8 times.
18 ----- 15 | 2835 -15 --- 133 -120 ← (15 × 8 = 120) --- 135
4 Subtract and bring down: 133 - 120 = 13. Bring down the 5 to make 135.
5 15 goes into 135: 15 × 9 = 135. Perfect match!
189 ----- 15 | 2835 -15 --- 133 -120 --- 135 -135 ← (15 × 9 = 135) ---- 0
6 Final Answer: 2,835 ÷ 15 = 189 with no remainder.
💡 Remember: The key steps in long division are:
1. Divide - How many times does the divisor go into the current number?
2. Multiply - Multiply the divisor by your answer
3. Subtract - Subtract to find the remainder
4. Bring Down - Bring down the next digit
5. Repeat - Keep going until there are no more digits!
